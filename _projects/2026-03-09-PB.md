## Motivation

A newly acquired 3D printer, a fresh ATmega2560, and an interest in robotics. As my Spring 2026 semester began, I aimed to use my limited free time in between classes, lab reports, and research to utilize these three things in one cohesive project. 

With this project, I created a simple yet intentional 3 DOF robotic arm that cost less than $30 to reproduce using Fusion360 for 3D modeling, unipolar stepper motors for movement, and an Arduino MEGA for controls. 

I found this to be an amazing project to get familiar with the entirety of the design process. From modeling a hole with a +0.2mm tolerance to fit a bearing and designing the four-bar linkage to not collide with the frame to making sure I supply the motor driver with correct signals, I gained a great deal of experience with all aspects of this system.
